What Should You Check Before Buying a Plot?

Before making a decision, buyers should carefully evaluate the location, road connectivity, surrounding development, available amenities, and legal documentation. It is also important to verify the project's approvals, ownership documents, layout plan, and applicable registration details.

Buyers should avoid making decisions based solely on the quoted price. The surrounding infrastructure, future development potential, accessibility, and reputation of the developer can also play an important role in determining the suitability of a property.
